Inward: Cell to Quark

Privacy policy and support

Privacy

Inward does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It works fully offline and does not connect to the internet.

The 360° inside view uses your device's motion sensors only to turn the camera in real time. This motion data never leaves your device and is not stored.

If you choose to share or save an image of a scene, the app asks for permission to add it to your photo library. It never reads your photos.
